# Content, attribution, and reuse

This collection is maintained by [Image 2.5](https://image-2-5.com). It links
creative work to its original source and explains how to reuse the workflow.

## What each entry means

- **Original prompt**: the published wording, preserved without rewriting.
- **Translation**: our translation of published wording, not a second original.
- **Editor adaptation**: our copyable recipe based on the cited workflow. It is
  labeled separately and is not claimed to reproduce the source image exactly.
- **Source checked**: a maintainer opened the primary source. This is not an
  independent model test or a license grant.
- **Independently tested**: a dated test with model, parameters, owned or
  authorized inputs, and output evidence is linked. No launch entry has this badge.

Social metrics are dated observations, not live rankings or quality guarantees.
Missing counts are `null`, never inferred as zero. Source posts may be paid
partnerships; known disclosures are retained in the record.

## Rights

The MIT license covers our tooling and original documentation. Community prompt
text and third-party media remain subject to their original rights and terms;
this project does not relicense them. Translations and adaptations do not erase
those underlying rights. Attribution alone is not permission to redistribute
someone else's work.

Launch previews are remotely linked to the creators' public posts and labeled
as source examples. They are not our generation results and are not bundled
into a new image dataset. Before copying or commercially reusing third-party
media, check the source's permission. For a durable local preview, contribute
your own output with its generation record or obtain the creator's permission.

When adding openly licensed material, record the exact license URL, author,
original source, and changes. Do not assume a gallery's umbrella license covers
every image it embeds. Keep source text separate from editorial text.

## Corrections and removal

Open a [content correction or removal issue](https://github.com/wangrunlin/awesome-gpt-image-2-5-prompts/issues/new?template=content.yml)
with the entry ID and source URL, or contact support@image-2-5.com for a private
request. Include only the information needed to identify the material. We will
review attribution, model labeling, permission, and removal requests.
