# Code of conduct

This is a welcoming place to share image-generation techniques, feedback, and
corrections. Treat contributors and creators with respect.

- Discuss the work constructively and credit other people's contributions.
- Do not harass, threaten, discriminate, spam, or disclose private information.
- Keep issues and pull requests relevant to this collection.
- Respect correction and attribution requests.

Maintainers may edit or remove disruptive contributions and restrict repeated
abuse. Report a concern privately to support@image-2-5.com; include only the
details needed to investigate it. This applies to project spaces and to people
representing the project in public.
